Official Legislative ABEST Findings & Major Policy Inflections (FY 2015 - FY 2026)

PACT Act & $9.45B Expansion

Passed in August 2022, the federal PACT Act expanded presumptive conditions, pushing Active Caseload to 366,854 and Service-Connected Monetary Awards to a record $9,445.27M ($9.45 Billion) in FY 2026 (188.9% of target).

Form vs. Issue Counting Shift

In FY22–FY23, TVC switched from counting multiple disability issues per claim to counting single claim forms in VetPro. This created an apparent paper drop to ~45k–49k until the Legislature recalibrated the FY26 target from 126k down to 45,000 (achieving 119.9%).

Appeals Modernization (AMA)

Following the 2017 AMA reforms, appeals shifted to Supplemental Claims and Higher-Level Reviews (Forms 10182, 20-0995, 20-0996), reaching 18,590 in FY23 before stabilizing as TVC focused on high first-time approval quality.

Strike Force Pivot to QC

The Strike Force evolved from "Beating the Backlog" to Post-Decision Quality Assurance reviews (with a daily standard of 7 reviews per specialist). The FY26 target was recalibrated to 5,000 reviews, with actuals reaching 17,655 (353.1%).
